# Ten-minute decomposition

For the first meeting about a deployment ask: "can the bot handle billing disputes?", "customers
are locked out, can AI help?". The goal is not a solution; it is the right first move and the
number that says whether it worked. Worked examples: the Decomp series at
[genai4business.com/writing](https://genai4business.com/writing).

## 1. The ask, restated

One sentence in the customer's words, then one in yours: who calls, about what, and what a good
outcome is for the caller and for the business. If the two outcomes differ, write both.

## 2. The flow today

Five to eight steps from the first contact to the outcome, including the human ones. Mark where
the time goes and where contacts come back.

| Step | Who | Volume or share (range) | Where it fails |
|---|---|---|---|

## 2b. Systems, data and owners

| System of record | Read or write | Access today (API, RPA, none) | Owner | Verification level needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Integration is where deployments slip: name the system nobody owns yet.

## 3. Outcomes and the metric that hides them

- The dashboard metric everyone watches (containment, AHT, deflection).
- The outcome it can hide: contained but not resolved, a repeat contact, a wrong action taken
  confidently, a vulnerable caller kept from a human.
- The metric you would add, and where its data comes from.

## 4. Risks that block, not just cost

Safety (emergencies, vulnerable callers), privacy (who is on the line, what they can see), money
(a wrong refund, a wrong reading billed), regulation. For each: the check that would stop a release.

## 5. Options, cheapest first

| Option | What it changes | What it costs | What it teaches in two weeks |
|---|---|---|---|

The cheapest first move is often not the agent: a form, a callback, a policy change, a better
handoff. Say why you would or would not start there.

## 6. The first move and its number

One move, one metric with a baseline and a target range, one date to look again, and the
assumption that would change your mind if it turned out wrong.
